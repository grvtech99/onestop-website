3. Recommended Final Flow

  1. Source Review — check changed or failed official sources.
  2. Draft Dashboard — inspect the discovered notice and extraction state.
  3. Review Queue — verify the official notice manually.
  4. Verify details — title, vacancy, dates, eligibility, fee, age, selection, notice PDF and application URL.
  5. Verified Drafts — confirm the verified record.
  6. Publication Center — create the publication PR through authenticated GitHub Actions.
  7. Owner approval + merge — publish only after the controlled PR is approved and merged.
  8. Public Job Updates — confirm the final entry renders correctly.
Launch rule: Keep testing limited to pipeline syntax/build, draft-only publication safety and the final live deployment check. Do not add repetitive test layers unless a production-risk issue requires them.
