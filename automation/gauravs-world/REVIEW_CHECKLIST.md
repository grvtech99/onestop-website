# Gaurav’s World — Editorial Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every candidate before it is copied into the blog. This pipeline is draft-first: completing this checklist does not publish anything.

## 1. Topic and sourcing
- [ ] Topic is relevant to the intended Gaurav’s World audience.
- [ ] Each factual claim that could change over time has a source URL.
- [ ] Source date and publication are recorded where available.
- [ ] The article distinguishes confirmed facts, attributed claims, and uncertainty.
- [ ] No statistic, quote, person, event, or citation has been invented.
- [ ] Sources are opened and checked; headlines alone are not treated as proof.

## 2. Hindi-first bilingual writing
- [ ] Hindi is the primary, complete explanation; English support is clear and accurate.
- [ ] Technical terms are explained simply on first use.
- [ ] Hindi and English sections agree on the facts and do not contradict each other.
- [ ] Tone is informative, accessible, and not sensationalized.

## 3. Originality and attribution
- [ ] Wording and structure are original, not copied from a source article.
- [ ] Any direct quotation is brief, accurate, and attributed.
- [ ] Source links appear in the draft in a readable “Sources” section.
- [ ] No copyrighted source image has been reused without permission.

## 4. Image review
- [ ] Image is newly generated or otherwise properly licensed.
- [ ] Image is illustrative and does not falsely depict a real event/person as documentary evidence.
- [ ] No misleading text, fake logos, fabricated screenshots, or false labels appear.
- [ ] Image dimensions, crop, alt text, and file format suit the blog.

## 5. Blog readiness
- [ ] Title is accurate and not clickbait.
- [ ] Slug is unique and URL-safe.
- [ ] Excerpt accurately summarizes the article.
- [ ] Headings, links, spelling, and mobile readability are checked.
- [ ] No private information, credentials, or unnecessary personal data is included.

## 6. Human approval and publication
- [ ] Reviewer has read the complete article and image.
- [ ] Corrections have been applied and sources rechecked.
- [ ] Reviewer explicitly approves this exact version for publication.
- [ ] Only after approval, publication may be considered through a verified, authenticated blog workflow.

## Decision
- [ ] APPROVE FOR PUBLICATION
- [ ] RETURN FOR REVISION
- [ ] REJECT
