How Verification Works

  1. Open the official source from the queue item.
  2. Find the actual new/updated notice, notification, result, admit card or other official document.
  3. Confirm the title, important dates, eligibility, vacancies and application/result details directly from that official notice.
  4. Only after verification should the information be manually added or updated in onestop-updates-data.js.
✔ Public Job Update data remains separate from this detection queue. This prevents automatic publication of incorrect or incomplete information.

Queue records are generated by the scheduled government-source checker. The current static site intentionally does not expose a public credential-bearing “Approve & Publish” button.
